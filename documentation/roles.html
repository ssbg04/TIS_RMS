Staff Roles & Access

Dedicated access controls for School Administrators and Classroom Teachers.

School Administrator

Registrar & Records Custodian
School-Wide

Unrestricted supervisory oversight across all grade levels, sections, and modules. Governs student enrollment status, staff provisioning, system policies, and compliance reporting.

  • Official Standing: Sole role authorized to update formal DepEd standing (Enrolled, Transferred, Dropped, Graduated).
  • System Governance: Configures academic years, required document checklists, and provisions staff accounts.
  • Compliance & Auditing: Full access to school-wide logs, DepEd dashboards, and Excel data exports.

Classroom Teacher

Class Adviser & Document Submitter
Advisory Scoped

Targeted day-to-day records management strictly scoped to assigned advisory sections. Handles new learner registration, biographical updates, and credential uploads.

  • Learner Registration: Register new students via OCR camera, manual intake, or CSV batch files.
  • Data & Documents: Update permitted bio-demographics (LRN, name, birth date, 4Ps) and upload SF9/PSA files.
  • Batch Processing: Queue documents and print official batches for assigned advisory learners.

TIS-RMS strictly enforces role-based access control (RBAC) across both client interfaces and backend APIs. Every action is recorded with audit timestamps and user identifiers. Review the granular permission matrix below:

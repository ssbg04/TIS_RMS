Talisay Integrated School - Records Management System

A user-friendly records management and document archiving system created for Talisay Integrated School. Easily manage learner information, keep track of required school documents, scan student papers, and print permanent records on Windows computers and Android tablets without needing an internet connection.

Windows Desktop Android Tablet Works Without Internet Live Updates Easy Batch Printing Learner Information Tracking

Staff Roles & Access

A clear guide to what Administrators and Teachers can view and do in the system.

Roles Overview
  • Administrator (Registrar): Full access to view all grade levels and sections, create staff accounts, manage document checklists, restore archived files, and save backups.
  • Teacher / Adviser: Dedicated access to view their assigned class sections, register new students, and upload scanned student documents.
  • Activity History: Automatically keeps a clear record of who viewed, added, or printed student records so files remain safe and organized.

The system makes it easy to collaborate while keeping student information safe and organized. Permissions are arranged so teachers can smoothly manage their advisory classes while school administrators oversee records across all grade levels.

Sign In & System Access

How to log into your account on desktop computers and Android tablets.

Sign-In Summary
  • Simple Sign-In: Sign in with the username and temporary password provided by your school administrator.
  • Automatic Workspace Setup: When you sign in, the system automatically opens the tools and sections you need based on your role.
  • School Wi-Fi Connection: Connect your tablet or desktop to the school's local Wi-Fi network to start working immediately.

Module 1: Dashboard & Overview

At-a-glance summaries, student counts, and reminders for missing documents.

Key Highlights
  • Helpful Summary Cards: Instant counts for Total Students, Students with Incomplete Requirements, Total Uploaded Documents, and Active Sections.
  • Live Updates: Counts update immediately on your screen whenever a teacher or staff member adds a student or uploads a document on another device.
  • Recent Activity Feed: A quick, easy-to-read list showing recent actions taken across the school.

The Dashboard is your main home screen. It gives teachers and administrators a clear view of student enrollment, quick reminders for incomplete documents, and recent file activities—all updated automatically in real time without needing an internet connection.

What You Can See on the Dashboard

Live Summary Counters

See the total count of enrolled students, archived records, uploaded files, and students who still need to submit documents.

Grade Level Distribution

Clear visual charts showing student numbers across Grade 7 through Grade 12 so teachers and administrators can see class sizes at a glance.

Recent Activity Feed

A continuous list of recent actions, such as newly registered students, uploaded documents, and printed records.

Module 2: Student Information System

Learner profiles, enrollment details, requirement status, and fast SF9/SF10 document scanning.

Key Highlights
  • 12-Digit LRN Tracking: Every student record is organized by their unique Learner Reference Number to prevent duplicate entries.
  • Quick Data Entry with SF9 & SF10 (OCR): When adding a new student, scan an SF9 (Form 138) or SF10 (Form 137) to automatically fill in basic form fields like Name, LRN, Birthday, and Sex.
  • Missing Document Reminders: A clear status badge shows right away if a student still needs to submit any required documents.

The Student Information module holds essential learner details in one convenient place. You can search for students quickly by typing their LRN, last name, first name, grade level, or section.

Step-by-Step: Adding a New Student

1

Open the Registration Form

Navigate to the Students screen and click Add Student, or press keyboard shortcut Ctrl+N on your desktop.

2

Enter Details or Scan SF9 / SF10 (OCR)

Type in the student's basic details: 12-digit LRN, full name, sex, birthday, grade, section, and 4Ps status. To save time, you can click Scan Document and scan their SF9 or SF10—the scanner will automatically read the document and fill in these text fields for you! (Note: The scanner only fills in the form text fields; it does not automatically attach the scanned file to the student's folder).

3

Automatic Folder Setup

The system creates a personal digital folder for the student with slots for required documents like their PSA Birth Certificate, SF9, Good Moral Certificate, and 2x2 Photo.

4

Save Record

Click Save Record. The student is saved immediately and becomes visible to staff across all connected school computers and tablets.

Module 3: Documents & Virtual Folders

Organized digital filing cabinets, flexible document requirements, and instant document viewing.

Key Highlights
  • Virtual Filing Cabinets: Neatly organizes student documents by School Year, Grade Level, and Section—just like a physical filing cabinet.
  • Dynamically Add, Edit, and Enable/Disable Documents: School administrators can add new document types anytime, edit existing document names, and turn specific requirements on or off as school policies change.
  • Instant Built-in Viewer: Click any document to open a full preview directly inside the app to read, zoom, and rotate pages without needing extra software.

TIS-RMS keeps student files neatly sorted and accessible. Instead of searching through paper filing cabinets, staff can find and view any student document in seconds. Document requirements are completely flexible—administrators can easily add new document types, rename them, or enable and disable requirements as needed.

Common Document Examples

PSA / NSO Birth Certificate

Confirms official learner legal name, date of birth, sex, and parent information.

DepEd Form 138 (SF9)

Report card copy kept in the student's folder for records tracking.

Certificate of Good Moral

Character certification required for student records, transfers, and graduation.

2x2 Student Identification Photo

Formal identification portrait used for official school records and certificates.

Module 4: Student Archives & Records Retention

Safe, long-term storage for graduated, transferred, and inactive student records.

Key Highlights
  • Separate Inactive Storage: Keeps graduated and transferred students separate from your active classes while keeping all their files permanently accessible.
  • Fast Archive Search: Quickly search and filter through past school years, grade levels, and sections to locate past records in seconds.
  • One-Click Restore: Administrators can restore any student back to an active section at any time with all their documents intact.

Student records must be kept permanently for future reference and verification. TIS-RMS stores completed student records safely in the Archives so your active student lists stay clean, while past records remain easy to find whenever a graduate requests their papers.

Archive Safeguards

Protected Records

Archived student profiles are safely locked to prevent accidental changes while preserving complete details.

Instant Restoration

Easily return a student to active enrollment if they return or were archived by accident, with all their documents intact.

Easy Group Filtering

Filter archived records by School Year (e.g., 2023–2024), Grade Level, Section, or Status (Graduated, Transferred Out, or Dropped).

Module 5: Batch Print Records

Print multiple student files or entire sections together in one easy step.

Key Highlights
  • Print Multiple Students at Once: Select several students or an entire section to print their records together in one go.
  • Smooth Background Preparation: The system prepares print jobs smoothly in the background so you can keep using the app without delays.
  • Ready for Official Submission: Prepares clean, professional printed copies ready for district audits, transfers, and graduation requirements.

Preparing physical documents for an entire class can take hours if done one by one. TIS-RMS lets you select an entire section and print their records in a single coordinated batch.

1

Select Students

On the Students or Archives screen, check the boxes for the students you wish to print, or click Select All in Section.

2

Open Batch Print

Click the Batch Print toolbar icon or press keyboard shortcut Ctrl+P on your desktop.

3

Choose Documents to Print

Select which documents you need for the selected students (such as SF10 forms or full student folder packages).

4

Send to Printer

Click Print to send the documents directly to your connected printer or save them as a ready-to-print file.

Module 6: Student Records & Forms

Standardized DepEd SF10 forms and student summary sheets focusing on essential learner information.

Key Highlights
  • Standard Form Layout: Generates clean SF10 forms and learner summary sheets with official school headers.
  • Core Learner Information Only: Strictly scopes essential student details: Full Name, LRN, Sex, Birthday, and 4Ps Beneficiary Status (Yes or No).
  • No Academic Grades or Remarks: The system is built for student identity and document tracking—it does not track, calculate, or manage grades, subjects, or teacher remarks.

The Student Records & Forms feature prepares official DepEd SF10 templates and student summary sheets. To keep records management focused and straightforward, the system scopes only basic student identification information and does not handle academic grades, subject lists, or promotional remarks.

Information Handled by the System

Learner Legal Name

Accurately records Last Name, First Name, Middle Name, and Name Extension (e.g., Jr., III).

12-Digit LRN

The official unique Learner Reference Number issued by DepEd.

Sex & Birthday

Verified gender and date of birth as stated in official PSA records.

4Ps Status (Yes or No)

Clear record indicating whether the student is a member of the 4Ps assistance program.

Module 7: User Accounts & Activity History

Manage staff accounts, set permissions, and see a clear history of system actions.

Key Highlights
  • Account Management: Administrators can easily add new staff members and assign their role as Administrator or Teacher.
  • Automatic Temporary Passwords: When creating an account or resetting a password, the system provides a secure temporary password displayed directly on screen.
  • Clear Activity History: A simple history log records who viewed, added, updated, or printed records so staff always know what was done.

Keeping student records safe is essential. TIS-RMS ensures each teacher and staff member has their own login, while maintaining a clear, readable activity history to protect school records.

Activity History Log

The History screen gives administrators a friendly, chronological summary of recent school records actions. It shows who performed each action, which student record was accessed, and what date and time it occurred.

Module 8: School Settings & Document Checklist

Customize school information, manage document requirements, and save backup copies.

Key Highlights
  • Dynamic Document Checklist: Add new document types, change names, and easily enable or disable requirements for each grade level anytime.
  • School Profile Headers: Set the School Name, School ID, District, Division, and Region for all printed forms.
  • Save Backup Copies: Create backup copies of your records anytime and save them to a USB flash drive for safekeeping.

The Settings screen gives Administrators full control over school preferences. You can update school details that appear on printed documents, customize which documents are required, and save backup copies of all records.

Managing Dynamic Document Requirements

Document requirements are never rigid. If the school introduces a new requirement, administrators can click Add Document and name it. You can edit existing document titles, or toggle Enabled / Disabled with one click. When a document is enabled and marked as required, the system will remind teachers if a student has not submitted it yet. If disabled, it is cleanly hidden from student checklists.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Helpful answers to common questions about using the system day-to-day.

Getting Started & Staff Logins

Teachers and staff are given their username and a temporary password by the school administrator. Simply type your username and password into the sign-in screen and click Log In.

Administrators open User Accounts, click Create User or select an existing staff account and click Reset Password. The system automatically creates a secure temporary password shown directly on the screen for the staff member to use.

School Network & Everyday Use

No. TIS-RMS runs completely inside the school's local network. You do not need internet access to add students, search records, view scanned documents, or print permanent forms.

Updates appear automatically in real time. Whenever a student is added, a document is uploaded, or information is updated on one computer, all other connected computers and tablets refresh their screens automatically without needing to reload.

Simply connect your Android tablet to the school's Wi-Fi network, open the TIS-RMS app, enter your login details, and tap Log In.

Student Records & Document Scanning

When adding a student, you can use the OCR scanner on an SF9 (Form 138) or SF10 (Form 137). The scanner automatically reads the document and populates the text fields on the "Add Student" form (such as Name, LRN, Birthday, and Sex) so you don't have to type them out by hand. Please note: OCR only populates these form text fields; it does not automatically upload or save the scanned file into the student's document folder.

Documents & Dynamic Requirements

The system accepts PDF documents as well as clear photos and scanned image files (such as JPG and PNG).

Printing & Saving Backups

On the Students or Archives screen, choose your section from the dropdown, click Select All in Section, and click Batch Print (Ctrl+P). The system prepares all the records into a single neat print package ready for your printer.

Administrators should save a backup regularly (for example, weekly or at the end of each grading period). Go to Settings, select Create Backup Copy, and save the backup file to a safe external USB drive.

Help & Troubleshooting

Helpful tips and quick solutions for common questions.

Connecting Your Tablet or Computer

Make sure your Android tablet or desktop computer is connected to the school's local Wi-Fi or office network. Once connected, open the app and sign in with your credentials.

Refreshing Document Previews

If a scanned document image takes a moment to appear or was recently re-uploaded, click Refresh Preview on the document card to reload the clear image.

Preparing for Batch Printing

Before printing records for an entire section, check that your school printer is turned on with enough paper and ink loaded so your print job finishes smoothly.
